## Verdict: PASS

**Summary:** The strongest quality is that the load-bearing claim — "branch I never reads PostgreSQL directly" — is made *executable* rather than asserted: §4.3 defines a versioned `contracts/client-interface-v1.json` manifest with a frozen `.lock`, and §5.2 backs it with four independent enforcement tests (`client_interface_manifest_entries_all_exist`, `client_interface_stable_entries_are_frozen`, `quickshell_sources_contain_no_database_access`, `tui_module_has_no_storage_dependency` + `tui_runs_entirely_against_a_fake_port`), all of which I verified are runnable in this repo's plain `cargo test` setup. §7.1's statement of current state is accurate on every checkable point. The most critical gap is an internal inconsistency in the one deliverable the spec claims is shippable today: §1.3 says the manifest and its contract test are "Available now … checked in and green," but 12 of the 13 §4.3 entries name CLI commands that do not exist, and §5.2 says such an entry "fails the build." The manifest schema has no field to express an entry that is specified but not yet implemented.
