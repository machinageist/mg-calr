## Verdict: PASS

**Summary:** The strongest quality is the identity/selector separation and the
credential-boundary discipline: §4.2's seven binding identity invariants plus the
`Resolution` enum with no first-match path, and §4.3/§5.1/§5.3's `safe_summary()`-only
display path backed by an adversarial `hunter2` process test, are both genuinely at the
"Excellent" anchor and both check out against the real code. The most critical gap is
factual, not architectural: the spec consistently misreports the migration inventory —
it claims five embedded migrations (§7.1, §4.2, §4.7) when `src/storage.rs:44-75` embeds
six, and it proposes `migrations/0006_audit_provenance.sql` at ledger version 6 (§4.1,
§4.2, §7.2) when `migrations/0006_repair_todo_recurrence.sql` already occupies that
filename and version. It also misdescribes its own migration runner (§3.2 step 8 knows
nothing about the checksum drift check or the hardcoded version-3 `ALTER COLUMN`).
None of these breach a pass condition, but the audit migration cannot be authored as
written and must be renumbered to 0007 before implementation.
